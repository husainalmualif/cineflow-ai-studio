## 1. Core Architecture, Model Compatibility & Credit Governance (`توافقية النماذج وحوكمة التكلفة`)

### 1.1 Model-Agnostic Execution (`توافقية النماذج المتعددة`)
- **Cross-Model Compatibility**: Engineered to execute natively across **Antigravity**, **Gemini 4 Argon (Task)**, **Claude**, and **Codex**. Users run the skill on whichever model is available in their current plan without needing multiple overlapping subscriptions.
- **Delegated vs. Directed Interaction (`أسلوب التعامل الذكي مع المستخدم`)**:
  - You are the **Creative Director & Technical Crew**. Never interrogate the user with long questionnaires. Make coherent creative decisions autonomously (style, palette, camera, motion, layout, sound) and state your chosen direction in one concise line.
  - Preserve any explicit user preference (`Directed`) and lock their exact spoken/written dialect (`Verbatim Script Law`).
- **Dual Execution Engines**:
  1. **Generative Studio (`Google Flow Ecosystem`)**: `Veo 3.1` (`Lite / Fast / Quality` — `4s, 6s, 8s` in `9:16` & `16:9`, up to `4K`, native audio & `[00:00-00:02]` timestamp sub-beats), `Gemini Omni Flash 1.1` (`360p` draft ➔ `0-credit 720p` upscale, `@Voice` lock), and `Nano Banana Pro / 2 Lite` (**`0-credit` keyframe & character/product reference sheets**).
  2. **Programmatic Post-Production Engine (`FFmpeg + Whisper + Remotion / Canvas`)**: Dual-threshold silence cutting with breath padding, word-level Arabic RTL kinetic captions, 4-state spatial layouts (`R_FULL / R_DOWN / R_LOWER / R_OFF`), Action-Verb Motion Stage (`stage`), 17 visual design styles, 100+ motion moves, `-14 LUFS` 4-bus audio mastering, CapCut project export, and interactive browser timeline editing.

### 1.2 Token & Credit Guardrails (`بند حوكمة وضبط التكلفة`)
1. **Token Optimization (`تقنين الأوامر وتوفير التوكنز`)**: Eliminate conversational filler, batch independent terminal operations into single background commands, inspect preview contact sheets (`6 frames per sheet`) rather than reading dozens of individual images, and load reference files only when needed.
2. **Separation of Planning & Render (`فصل التخطيط عن التوليد الفعلي`)**: Execute all scriptwriting, creative divergence, cut planning, and QA checks in text/JSON (`Text-to-Text / Logic Operations`) before triggering any expensive visual render inside **Google Flow**.
3. **3-Stage Google Flow Credit Workflow**:
   - **Stage 1 (`0 Credits`)**: Generate character/product reference sheets and keyframes using **`Nano Banana 2 Lite`**.
   - **Stage 2 (`Half Credit + Free Upscale`)**: Test motion in **`Gemini Omni Flash 1.1` (`360p` ➔ `0-credit 720p` upscale)**.
   - **Stage 3 (`Master Render`)**: Render final approved shots in **`Veo 3.1 Quality` (`1080p / 4K`)**.
4. **Micro-Scene Efficiency (`معيار المشاهد القصيرة الذكية`)**: Restrict viral short-form edits to **10 to 15 seconds total** (or **5s to 8s**, max `10s` per shot) to prevent `AI Visual Degradation` and eliminate costly re-generations.

## 2. Master Video Classification & 6 Production Routes (`الهيكل التصنيفي ومسارات الإنتاج الستة`)

### A. By Purpose (`حسب الغرض والهدف — 8 مسارات`)
1. **`Commercials & TVCs` (الفيديوهات التجارية والإعلانية)**: Direct product/service promotion following the 6-stage Direct-Response curve (`0–2s Hook ➔ Problem Agitation ➔ Product Hero Showcase ➔ Social Proof ➔ Offer Stack ➔ Safe-Zone CTA`).
2. **`Corporate Videos` (الفيديوهات المؤسسية)**: Brand identity, corporate values, executive storytelling, and internal training.
3. **`Educational & Training` (الفيديوهات التعليمية والتدريبية)**: Knowledge simplification, structured tutorials, and visual analogies.
4. **`Explainer Videos` (فيديوهات الشرح وتبسيط المنتجات والتطبيقات)**: `Problem ➔ Agitation ➔ Solution` mechanics, UI/UX walkthroughs, and `/exploded_view` product breakdowns.
5. **`Testimonial Videos` (فيديوهات الشهادات والتوصيات)**: Customer stories and trust-building proof using eye-level `50mm/85mm` shallow depth-of-field interleaved with B-roll proof.
6. **`Documentaries` (الأفلام الوثائقية)**: Realistic narrative storytelling and historical/real-world documentation (`ARRI/RED Anamorphic`, `35mm film grain`, `map-route` & `claim-proof` overlays).
7. **`Event Videos` (فيديوهات الفعاليات والتغطيات)**: Live coverage energy capture and high-tempo Sizzle Reels.
8. **`Entertainment Videos` (فيديوهات الترفيه، السينما، البرامج، والأغاني المصورة)**: Narrative short films, sketches, episodic shows, and music videos.

### B. By Format (`حسب الأسلوب والشكل الفني — 5 أنماط`)
- **`Live-Action` (التصوير الحي الواقعي)** | **`Animation (2D / 3D)` (الرسوم المتحركة)** | **`Motion Graphics` (الموشن جرافيك)** | **`Live Streaming` (البث المباشر)** | **`360 & Interactive` (الفيديو التفاعلي و360 درجة)**.

### C. By Rhythmic Montage & Edits (`أسلوب المونتاج الإيقاعي والتجميعي — 5 أساليب`)
- **`Montage Sequence` (المونتاج السينمائي الدرامي)** | **`Music Videos` (الأغاني المصورة المتزامنة)** | **`Commercial Promo` (الإعلانات الحركية عالية الأدرينالين)** | **`Short-Form Edits / AMV` (المونتاج القصير الفيروسي 10–15s)** | **`Sizzle Reel / Recap` (ملخص الفعاليات الحركي)**.

### D. The 6 Autonomous Execution Routes (`مسارات التنفيذ والمونتاج الستة`)
Automatically route any user input to the right pipeline (see [`references/programmatic_editing_and_motion_guide.md`](./references/programmatic_editing_and_motion_guide.md)):
1. **`Generative AI Cinema Studio`**: Idea/Script ➔ `Google Flow` multi-scene video with `@Character`/`@Product` Lock & `Scene Handshake`.
2. **`Talking-Head Viral Reel & Ad Editor`**: Camera recording ➔ Dropped-frame & HDR repair ➔ Dual-threshold silence cut (`-32dB, 0.35s` + `120ms/150ms` breath padding) ➔ Whisper hallucination check & dialect fix ➔ 4-state layout (`R_FULL / R_DOWN / R_LOWER / R_OFF`) + Depth Moments + RTL captions.
3. **`Action-Verb Motion Stage & 60fps Motion Film`**: Text/Script ➔ 60fps Canvas/Remotion stage where every sentence's **verb** (`تجميع، تحويل، استبدال، نمو، خطر، مقارنة`) is drawn via animated actors (`3–6 actors`) anchored to exact spoken words (`word`, `toWord`, `pulseWord`).
4. **`Beat-Synced Silent B-Roll Montage`**: Folder of clips ➔ Multi-clip quality scan (`Clarity × Motion × Exposure × Color`, trimming first/last `0.33s` camera shake) ➔ Beat/Onset grid cut (`[1.0, 0.82, 1.24, 0.94]` cadence).
5. **`Multi-Cam Podcast & Landscape-to-Vertical Clipper`**: `16:9` episode ➔ Face-tracked `9:16` crop (`facetrack`) + mandatory 3-variant Hook Banner from strongest quote.
6. **`3D Device & Web/App Explainer`**: URL or screen capture ➔ `1080x1220` safe-zone framing (`<= 1.25x` zoom), pointer click ripples, highlight callouts, and 3D iPhone/MacBook framing.

## 3. Mandatory Production, Directorial & Editing Guardrails (`الضوابط الإخراجية والتقنية والمونتاجية الحرجة`)

Specify and enforce these **12 guardrails** across every blueprint and scene:

1. **Runtime Limits per Platform (`محددات الطول والوقت`)**:
   - **Short-Form Viral (`10–15s` total | `5–8s` per shot, max `10s`)** to prevent `AI Degradation` and maximize retention.
   - **Long-Form Narratives (`> 60s` YouTube / Vodcast)**: Partition into `60s–90s` chapters decomposed into `<= 10s` A-roll/B-roll shots.
2. **Tone of Voice (`نبرة الصوت والانفعال البشري`)**:
   - `Angry (غاضب)`, `Laughing/Joyful (ضاحك)`, `Weeping/Emotional (باكي)`, `Serious/Authoritative (جاد)`, `Cunning (ماكر)`, `Contemplative (متأمل)`, `Sarcastic (هازئ)`, or `Warm/Confident (دافئ/واثق)`.
3. **Body Language (`لغة الجسد الفيزيائية`)**:
   - 100% physical behavioral cues (**Physicality Firewall**): `Relaxed (مرتاح)`, `Tense (متوتر)`, `Deceptive (كاذب)`, `Confident (واثق)`, `Hesitant (متردد)`, `Friendly (ودود)`.
4. **Camera Gear, Anti-AI Sheen & Skin Protection (`نوع الكاميرا وحماية البشرة`)**:
   - **Cinema Emulation**: `Canon C500 Mark II + 85mm f/1.4`, `Sony Venice 2`, `RedMagic Film Camera`, `ARRI Alexa 65 + 35mm Anamorphic`, `RED V-Raptor 8K`, or `Phone Style — iPhone 16 Pro UGC`.
   - **Anti-AI Sheen (Generative)**: Inject `35mm film grain ISO 800, subtle optical halation, microscopic skin pores and authentic fabric texture --no CGI, no 3D render, no plastic skin, no AI sheen, no garbled Arabic text, no extra fingers, no identity drift`.
   - **Real Footage Skin Lock (`grade: false`)**: Never apply artificial color tints or blurs onto a real presenter's face; apply theme colors strictly to motion cards and typography.
5. **Camera Movements, Slash Macros & Dynamic Zoom (`حركة الكاميرا والزوم`)**:
   - Explicit vectors (`Pan, Tilt, Zoom, Dolly, Crane, Orbit, Whip-Pan, FPV Drone`) + **180+ `/macros`** (see [`references/cinematic_macros_and_continuity.md`](./references/cinematic_macros_and_continuity.md)) + locked per-cut zoom punch-ins (`1.0x <-> 1.12x–1.18x`) in talking-head edits.
6. **Color Grading, 8 Palettes & 17 Visual Styles (`التناسق اللوني وبنك الستايلات`)**:
   - **Color Grading**: `Cinematic`, `Warm Golden`, `Realistic Rec.709`, `Natural Colors`, or `Moody Dark Chiaroscuro`.
   - **8 Motion Palettes**: `midnight`, `ocean`, `sunset`, `emerald`, `gold`, `neon`, `mono`, `paper` (or custom brand hex `colors`).
   - **17 Visual Design Styles**: `keynote`, `glassUI`, `explainer`, `paperCut`, `archive`, `editorial`, `swiss`, `doodle`, `terminal`, `riso`, `newsprint`, `blueprint`, `vhs` (moment only), `brutal` (moment only), `studio3D`, `stickerToon`, `devCard`, or fused `A+B` styles (`explainer+keynote`).
7. **Character, Product & Scene Handshake Lock (`ثبات الهوية والمصافحة البصرية`)**:
   - Lock recurring assets via `Lock [Character ID: @Character_01]`, `Lock [Product ID: @Product_01]`, and `Lock [Voice ID: @Voice: Name]`.
   - Chain consecutive shots via **Scene Handshake**: save the final frame (`Save frame` / `ffmpeg -sseof -0.04`) of `Scene N` and feed it as `+ Add start frame` (`<FIRST_FRAME>`) of `Scene N+1`.
8. **4-State Spatial Layout & Calmness Rules (`قاعدة التخطيط الرباعية وحدود الهدوء`)**:
   - Alternate cleanly between **`R_FULL`** (full-screen presenter), **`R_DOWN`** (top graphic + bottom presenter cropped `1.5%` above head `cardTop`), **`R_LOWER` + `Headout`** (top 2/3 board + small bottom presenter with head popping out of frame & `nocap: true`), and **`R_OFF`** (full-screen visual stage takeover).
   - **Calmness & Hold Laws**: Leave `>= 2.0s` of `R_FULL` speaker between graphics; max `5` graphics per `30s`; no graphic before `t = 1.5s`; and hold every graphic/caption **`+0.45s` after the last spoken word** before easing out over `0.4s` (`e = last_word_end + 0.45s`).
9. **2–3 Signature Depth Moments & Strict Arabic Kashida Law (`لحظات العمق وقواعد الكشيدة`)**:
   - Plan **2–3 signature depth moments** per video: **3-Layer Stacked Hook (`stack`)**, **Behind-Head Kashida Text (`behind_text`)**, **Voice-Reactive Waveform (`MV.env`)**, **Brand-Colored Wall Illumination (`brandLight`)**, **3D Orbit (`orbit`)**, or **Spark Emblem Transition (`sparkReveal`)**.
   - **Strict Arabic Kashida Rules**: Insert Kashida (`ـ`) ONLY after connecting letters (never after `ا أ إ آ د ذ ر ز و ؤ ة ء ى`), place it **after** any Harakat/Tashkeel (`تحـــرّك`, never `تحرّـــك`), never split `لا`, stretch by pixel slice (never character count), and keep all letters outside the head so only the Kashida bar passes behind the speaker's head.
10. **Text, Facts Ledger & RTL Kinetic Caption Integrity (`سلامة النصوص والكابشن العربي`)**:
    - Lock diegetic packaging text via `@Product_01`; verify all on-screen numbers/names in a **Facts Ledger (`facts.md`)**; never animate Arabic letter-by-letter; never place captions on the speaker's face; and support `chunk` (4-word single-line capsule at `Y: 1395`), `word`, `card`, `shadow`, `bar`, or `capStill: true` luxury mode.
11. **Advanced Audio-Sync, Lip-Sync & `-14 LUFS` Mastering (`هندسة الصوت ومزامنة الشفاه`)**:
    - **Lip-Sync Audio-Math Law**: Enforce `Words <= Duration_Seconds * 2.5` and split Arabic/Gulf dialogue into short `3–5 word` phrases separated by `.. [0.3s breath pause]` (see [`references/scriptwriting_and_lip_sync_guide.md`](./references/scriptwriting_and_lip_sync_guide.md)).
    - **4-Bus Audio Mastering**: Normalize to **`-14.0 LUFS`** (`True Peak <= -1.0 to -1.5 dBTP`), auto-duck background audio (`bg-audio.mp3`) via `sidechaincompress` (`attack=15ms, release=450ms`), and limit SFX to `<= 15` meaningful state-change events/min (`peak < -18 dBFS`).
12. **Standardized Safe-Zone Outro & Mandatory First-Frame Cover (`الخاتمة المعيارية والغلاف الآمن`)**:
    - **Outro Safe Zone (`9:16` `1080x1920`)**: Reserve `Y: 420..820` for Brand Logo and `Y: 950..1280, X: 120..900` for CTA/Handles (avoiding Top `Y: 0..220`, Bottom `Y: 1500..1920`, and Right `X: 920..1080`).
    - **First-Frame Cover (`0.1s` Start Frame)**: Place face, 2-line hook (`<= 5 words`, `Headline >= 170px`, `Subtext >= 110px`), and logos (`>= 200px`) inside the central **`1080x1080` Safe Square (`Y: 420..1500`)** so nothing is clipped in `9:16`, `4:5`, or `1:1` grids.
