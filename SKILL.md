## 3. Mandatory Production, Directorial, Editing & Governance Guardrails (`الضوابط الإخراجية والتقنية والمونتاجية والحوكمة`)

Specify and enforce these **15 guardrails** across every blueprint and scene:

1. **Runtime Limits per Platform (`محددات الطول والوقت`)**:
   - **Short-Form Viral (`10–15s` total | `5–8s` per shot, max `10s`)** to prevent `AI Degradation` and maximize retention.
   - **Long-Form Narratives (`> 60s` YouTube / Vodcast)**: Partition into `60s–90s` chapters decomposed into `<= 10s` A-roll/B-roll shots.
2. **CMU/Harvard CHAI 5-Aspect Video Language (`اللغة البصرية الخماسية الإلزامية`)**:
   - Populate all 5 slots per scene: **`[1. Subject]`** (repeat 3–6 visual attributes **verbatim** in every shot; name subject transitions `revealing / disappearing / switching / complex-alternating` + cause), **`[2. Subject Motion]`** (temporal order; replace emotional adjectives with physical causes), **`[3. Scene]`** (**list overlays separately** from FG/MG/BG depth + POV + setting + dynamics), **`[4. Spatial Framing]`** (shot size, depth, camera height & angle: `bird's-eye = strict 90° top-down ≠ aerial`), and **`[5. Camera]`** (strictly separate **Translation** `dolly/truck/pedestal` vs. **Rotation** `pan/tilt/roll` vs. **Lens-Only** `zoom/rack focus/pull focus/focus tracking` vs. **Strict Stillness** `static`).
3. **Tone of Voice & Physical Body Language (`نبرة الصوت ولغة الجسد الفيزيائية`)**:
   - **Tone**: `Angry (غاضب)`, `Laughing/Joyful (ضاحك)`, `Weeping/Emotional (باكي)`, `Serious/Authoritative (جاد)`, `Cunning (ماكر)`, `Contemplative (متأمل)`, `Sarcastic (هازئ)`, or `Warm/Confident (دافئ/واثق)`.
   - **Body Language (Physicality Firewall)**: 100% physical cues (`Relaxed`, `Tense`, `Deceptive`, `Confident`, `Hesitant`, `Friendly`).
4. **Camera Gear, Anti-AI Sheen & Real Skin Protection (`نوع الكاميرا وحماية البشرة`)**:
   - **Cinema Emulation**: `Canon C500 Mark II + 85mm f/1.4`, `Sony Venice 2`, `RedMagic Film Camera`, `ARRI Alexa 65 + 35mm Anamorphic`, `RED V-Raptor 8K`, or `Phone Style — iPhone 16 Pro UGC`.
   - **Anti-AI Sheen (Generative)**: Inject `35mm film grain ISO 800, subtle optical halation, microscopic skin pores and authentic fabric texture --no CGI, no 3D render, no plastic skin, no AI sheen, no garbled Arabic text, no subtitles, no captions, no text overlays, no extra fingers, no identity drift`.
   - **Real Footage Skin Lock (`grade: false`)**: Never apply artificial color tints or blurs onto a real presenter's face; apply theme colors strictly to motion cards and typography.
5. **Camera Movements, 180+ Slash Macros & Dynamic Zoom (`حركة الكاميرا والزوم`)**:
   - Explicit vectors + **180+ `/macros`** (see [`references/cinematic_macros_and_continuity.md`](./references/cinematic_macros_and_continuity.md)) + locked per-cut zoom punch-ins (`1.0x <-> 1.12x–1.18x`) in talking-head edits.
6. **Color Grading, Taste Profile (`taste_profile`), 8 Palettes & 17 Visual Styles**:
   - **Taste Profile**: Lock `design_read` + 3 integer dials (`visual_variance: 1..10`, `motion_intensity: 1..10`, `information_density: 1..10`) + `anti_patterns`.
   - **8 Motion Palettes**: `midnight`, `ocean`, `sunset`, `emerald`, `gold`, `neon`, `mono`, `paper`.
   - **17 Visual Design Styles**: `keynote`, `glassUI`, `explainer`, `paperCut`, `archive`, `editorial`, `swiss`, `doodle` (`ink-sketch`), `terminal`, `riso`, `newsprint`, `blueprint`, `vhs` (moment only), `brutal` (moment only), `studio3D`, `stickerToon`, `devCard`, or fused `A+B` styles (`explainer+keynote`).
7. **Character, Product & Scene Handshake Lock (`ثبات الهوية والمصافحة البصرية`)**:
   - Lock recurring assets via `Lock [Character ID: @Character_01]`, `Lock [Product ID: @Product_01]`, and `Lock [Voice ID: @Voice: Name]`.
   - Chain consecutive shots via **Scene Handshake**: save the final frame (`Save frame` / `ffmpeg -sseof -0.04`) of `Scene N` and feed it as `+ Add start frame` (`<FIRST_FRAME>`) of `Scene N+1`.
8. **Dual Composition Runtimes (`Remotion` vs. `HyperFrames`) & Bespoke `Atelier` Doctrine**:
   - Lock `render_runtime` (`remotion` | `hyperframes` | `ffmpeg`) and `composition_mode` (`atelier` default for hero work | `templated` for batch/drafts) in `decision_log`.
   - **No-Hero-Component-Spine Law**: Every scene must have a distinct primary visual subject; the piece's signature device appears in at most 1–2 climactic beats.
   - **HyperFrames Stock-Footage Fix**: Pre-transform stock clips with **Scale-to-COVER + Unsharp + Dense Keyframes** (`scale=1920:1080:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=1920:1080,unsharp=3:3:0.6 -g 30 -keyint_min 30`) to prevent `640x360` letterbox traps and parallel Chrome freezes.
9. **4-State Spatial Layout & Calmness Rules (`قاعدة التخطيط الرباعية وحدود الهدوء`)**:
   - Alternate cleanly between **`R_FULL`** (full-screen presenter), **`R_DOWN`** (top graphic + bottom presenter cropped `1.5%` above head `cardTop`), **`R_LOWER` + `Headout`** (top 2/3 board + small bottom presenter with head popping out of frame & `nocap: true`), and **`R_OFF`** (full-screen visual stage takeover).
   - **Calmness & Hold Laws**: Leave `>= 2.0s` of `R_FULL` speaker between graphics; max `5` graphics per `30s`; no graphic before `t = 1.5s`; and hold every graphic/caption **`+0.45s` after the last spoken word** before easing out over `0.4s` (`e = last_word_end + 0.45s`).
10. **2–3 Signature Depth Moments & Strict Arabic Kashida Law (`لحظات العمق وقواعد الكشيدة`)**:
    - Plan **2–3 signature depth moments** per video: **3-Layer Stacked Hook (`stack`)**, **Behind-Head Kashida Text (`behind_text`)**, **Voice-Reactive Waveform (`MV.env`)**, **Brand-Colored Wall Illumination (`brandLight`)**, **3D Orbit (`orbit`)**, or **Spark Emblem Transition (`sparkReveal`)**.
    - **Strict Arabic Kashida Rules**: Insert Kashida (`ـ`) ONLY after connecting letters (never after `ا أ إ آ د ذ ر ز و ؤ ة ء ى`), place it **after** any Harakat/Tashkeel (`تحـــرّك`, never `تحرّـــك`), never split `لا`, stretch by pixel slice (never character count), and keep all letters outside the head so only the Kashida bar passes behind the speaker's head.
11. **Text, Facts Ledger, Caption-Dedup & RTL Kinetic Caption Integrity (`سلامة النصوص والكابشن العربي`)**:
    - Lock diegetic packaging text via `@Product_01`; verify all on-screen numbers/names in a **Facts Ledger (`facts.md`)**; never echo an on-screen `SerifLine` headline in auto-captions simultaneously (`caption_onscreen_text_dedup: true`); never animate Arabic letter-by-letter; never place captions on the speaker's face; and support `chunk` (4-word single-line capsule at `Y: 1395`), `word`, `card`, `shadow`, `bar`, or `capStill: true` luxury mode.
12. **Advanced Audio-Sync, Lip-Sync & `-14 LUFS` Mastering (`هندسة الصوت ومزامنة الشفاه`)**:
    - **Lip-Sync Audio-Math Law**: Enforce `Words <= Duration_Seconds * 2.5` and split Arabic/Gulf dialogue into short `3–5 word` phrases separated by `.. [0.3s breath pause]` (see [`references/scriptwriting_and_lip_sync_guide.md`](./references/scriptwriting_and_lip_sync_guide.md)).
    - **4-Bus Audio Mastering**: Normalize to **`-14.0 LUFS`** (`True Peak <= -1.0 to -1.5 dBTP`), auto-duck background audio (`bg-audio.mp3`) via `sidechaincompress` (`attack=15ms, release=450ms`), and limit SFX to `<= 15` meaningful state-change events/min (`peak < -18 dBFS`).
13. **Slideshow Risk Scorer (`score_slideshow_risk`) & Delivery Promise Enforcement**:
    - Score every scene plan across 6 dimensions (`repetition, decorative_visuals, weak_motion, weak_shot_intent, typography_overreliance, unsupported_cinematic_claims`; must achieve `< 3.0` verdict `strong` or `acceptable`) and enforce `motion_ratio >= 70%` on `motion_led` promises.
14. **CHAI 3-Axis Critique & 5-Point Post-Render Self-Review (`final_review`)**:
    - Every review critique must be **Accurate** (cite field/frame), **Complete** (pattern-wide scan), and **Constructive** (every `CRITICAL` finding must propose a concrete fix). Verify all 5 post-render checks (`technical_probe`, `visual_spotcheck >= 4 frames`, `audio_spotcheck`, `promise_preservation`, `subtitle_check`).
15. **Standardized Safe-Zone Outro & Mandatory First-Frame Cover (`الخاتمة المعيارية والغلاف الآمن`)**:
    - **Outro Safe Zone (`9:16` `1080x1920`)**: Reserve `Y: 420..820` for Brand Logo and `Y: 950..1280, X: 120..900` for CTA/Handles (avoiding Top `Y: 0..220`, Bottom `Y: 1500..1920`, and Right `X: 920..1080`).
    - **First-Frame Cover (`0.1s` Start Frame)**: Place face, 2-line hook (`<= 5 words`, `Headline >= 170px`, `Subtext >= 110px`), and logos (`>= 200px`) inside the central **`1080x1080` Safe Square (`Y: 420..1500`)** so nothing is clipped in `9:16`, `4:5`, or `1:1` grids.
